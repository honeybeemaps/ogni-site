Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 May 2026

This policy is provided as a general template and is not legal advice. Because Ogni is available to users in the EU and UK, consider having a lawyer review it.

The short version

Ogni does not collect your data. The habits you create and the times you mark them kept are stored on your device. They are never sent to us. We have no server, no account system, and no analytics tracking you.

What Ogni stores

Everything Ogni records stays on your iPhone:

  • Your habits — the names you type, their rhythm (daily / weekly / monthly), and how often you want to do them.
  • Your completions — the timestamps when you mark a habit kept, and any optional note you attach.
  • Your preferences — small settings such as your light/dark theme choice.

This information lives in Ogni's local storage on your device. We — the makers of Ogni — never receive it and cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • We do not have user accounts. There is no sign-up, no email, no password.
  • We do not run analytics or behavioral tracking. No third-party SDKs are embedded in the app.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data — because we never receive it in the first place.

iCloud sync (a future version)

A future version of Ogni will offer optional sync across your Apple devices using CloudKit. If and when that ships, your data would sync through your own private iCloud account — Apple stores it in the private database tied to your Apple ID, which only you can access. It would still never reach us. This policy will be updated before that feature is released.

Crash and diagnostic reports

If you have opted in to sharing diagnostics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements on your iPhone), Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymized crash and performance reports. These help us fix bugs. They do not contain your habit data or anything that identifies you. You can turn this off at any time in iOS Settings.

Your rights

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • Access — open the app; everything Ogni knows is visible there.
  • Correction — edit any habit at any time from its detail screen.
  • Deletion — delete any habit (with its full history) using "delete forever," or delete the Ogni app entirely to remove all of its data from your device.

If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR / UK GDPR grant you rights of access, rectification, erasure, and portability. For Ogni these are satisfied directly in the app, since we hold no copy of your data.

Data retention

We retain nothing — we have nothing to retain. Your data persists on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Children

Ogni is not directed to children under 13. Do not use Ogni if you are under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy — for example, when optional iCloud sync ships — we will update the "last updated" date above and, where the change is material, note it in the app.
